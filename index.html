THE MISSION

Old games had heart. Tight gameplay, unforgettable characters, and fun you could feel in five minutes. Dexstorm Gaming exists to bring that magic back — faithfully restored and remastered so today's kids can fall in love with it too.

👾

Restore

Classic genres and timeless gameplay loops — creature battlers, tycoons, space adventures — rebuilt with modern polish and respect for the originals.

🎮

Remaster

Hand-tuned pixel art, procedural music, juice-filled animation, and smooth modern controls. The games you remember, better than you remember them.

🧒

Reconnect

Made for new kids picking up their first controller — simple to learn, hard to put down, and full of the wonder that made us gamers in the first place.

THE FOUNDER

JOEY SANCHEZ — RETIRED U.S. NAVY CHIEF

After a career of service as a Chief Petty Officer in the United States Navy, Joey traded the deck plates for a dev workstation. He's now a solo game developer based in Caldwell, Idaho, building every Dexstorm title himself — code, art direction, music, and design.

His goal is simple: restoring classic games for kids today. The classics he grew up on taught him creativity and grit; now he's rebuilding that magic for the next generation, one pixel at a time.
